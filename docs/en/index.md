# Racing Pigeon Breeding and Lineage Encyclopedia

> **Key takeaway**: This encyclopedia covers the three core techniques of racing pigeon **lineage verification** — **paternity testing, sex identification and inbreeding coefficient analysis** — explaining their scientific principles, testing workflow and breeding applications. Together with the genetic ID card and AB pigeon identification, it helps fanciers move from "judging by experience" to data-driven lineage management.

Welcome to the **Racing Pigeon Breeding and Lineage Encyclopedia**. Compiled by [Hebei Sanshi Biotechnology Co., Ltd.](https://sanshibio.net/), it focuses on the core lineage questions in pigeon breeding: who are this bird's parents ([paternity testing](paternity-test.md)), is this bird male or female ([sex identification](sex-identification.md)), and how inbred is it ([inbreeding coefficient testing](inbreeding-coefficient.md))? All three techniques share one **SNP locus testing system**, so a single feather sample can answer several questions at once.

## FAQ

### Where should I start with racing pigeon lineage testing?

Start with [paternity testing](paternity-test.md) to understand how a blood relationship is verified by DNA, then read [sex identification](sex-identification.md) and [inbreeding coefficient testing](inbreeding-coefficient.md) to form a complete picture of lineage testing.

### Can the three tests be done together?

Yes. All three techniques share the same SNP locus testing system, so one feather sample completes them all — efficient and economical.

### Does sampling require a blood draw?

No. Pulling 4–6 feathers with the follicle attached provides enough DNA. Squabs can be sampled and the bird is barely affected.

### What is the difference between a pedigree and DNA lineage testing?

A pedigree can be altered or faked, while DNA lineage testing rests on genetic evidence that cannot be forged. Only the two together make a lineage both traceable and trustworthy.

### Can test results be used as a diagnosis?

Results are an **aid to judgement** and are not a medical diagnosis. For decisions on treatment, consult your veterinarian or the [Sanshi Bio technical team](https://sanshibio.net/services/).
